Phase 1 · ~50 minutes · no AI

Human research

You'll research the ecosystem around a dataset — what the news, the federal government, and social media are saying about it — and rate its risk against the rubric. You are not trying to become an expert in the dataset itself.

1. Pick your dataset

This choice is locked in for the whole exercise — Phase 3 carries it over automatically and builds its AI prompts from it.

2. Pick your categories

The rubric has six categories. In timed sessions each participant takes one triad of three; if you're working async and have time, you may attempt all six.

3. Rules of engagement

Do not

  • Look at America's Data Index. That's pretty much cheating — it's the comparison point for the very end.
  • Use AI in this phase — chatbots, and to the extent you can, AI-generated search summaries. If an AI Overview is unavoidable, don't rely on it: click through to sources.
  • Worry too much about time. Not being able to assess a category in the time you have is itself valuable information — record that and move on.

Do

  • Look anywhere else: social media, government websites, news, trade press, paywalled material.
  • Research the ecosystem: what are the news, the fed, and social media saying about this data and its agency?
  • Do your best to cover all your assigned categories, even if it means moving on before you feel ready.
  • Keep rough notes on which kinds of sources you used — the assessment form asks.

4. Your rubric and worksheet

Assess each of your categories at one of four levels, and record your rating and evidence as you go. Full six-category rubric on the rubric page.

Everything you type below is saved in this browser and carried into Phase 3 automatically — you won't be asked to re-enter any of it. Nothing is sent anywhere until you submit the form in step 5.

5. Submit your assessment

When you're done (or out of time), complete the Researcher Assessment Form. For each category, give a risk level and briefly note the evidence behind it — "couldn't assess" is a legitimate answer. Your worksheet above has everything the form asks for; copy it across.

What happens next

If you're participating asynchronously, you're done — thank you. Our automated harness (Phase 2) assesses the same dataset with no human steering, and we compare. If you're in a synchronous session, continue to Phase 3.
