Phase 2 · team-run · nothing for you to do

The automated benchmark

There's no work for you in this phase — it has already happened. Ahead of your session, we ran an AI agent on the same assignment you were given: the same dataset metadata, the same rubric, the same output format, with zero human steering. Its assessment is already written and held back for comparison.

How the harness works

  • Same inputs. The agent receives the dataset's title, publisher, description, and URL, plus the risk-assessment prompt and rubric — essentially the Phase 3 prompts with no human in the loop.
  • Agent-agnostic. The framework is designed so we can point it at different models and agents and compare across them.
  • Reasoning is logged. The agent records its thinking as it works, so we can study its process, not just its verdicts — the same way the assessment form asks humans which sources they used.

That's the whole idea to take away here: an AI has already assessed your dataset in the background, unprompted and unassisted. Its answers stay sealed until the debrief so that your assessment and its assessment stay independent.
