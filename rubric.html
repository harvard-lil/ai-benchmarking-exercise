Assessment rubric

Six categories of risk to a federal dataset, each rated at one of four levels. The rubric was created by data policy experts; humans and the AI agents are given identical wording.

Terms: ICR = Information Collection Request, the OMB clearance a federal agency needs to collect data from the public. An expired ICR is a leading indicator that collection may lapse. TEAM DECISION #8 — whether/where to define more legal terms (statute, regulation) is not settled.
